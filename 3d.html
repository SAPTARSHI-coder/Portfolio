Academic Output

Research & Manuscripts

Manuscript in Prep (2026)

Ozone Photochemical Regimes and Particulate Transport in Kolkata and Howrah

Argha Kamal Guha, Saptarshi Sadhu, Chirag Deb

Analyzed ground-level ozone formation, precursor titration dynamics (NOx/VOC), and PM2.5 transport corridors across urban airsheds.

Manuscript in Prep (2026)

A Statistical Distribution, Spatial Autocorrelation, and Recurrent Neural Network Framework for Air Quality Forecasting in Kolkata--Howrah

Argha Kamal Guha, Saptarshi Sadhu, Susmit Bagchi, Chirag Deb

Proposed a multi-tier PyTorch LSTM recurrent neural network and Moran's I spatial autocorrelation framework evaluated on ~19.1 million 15-minute CAAQMS data points ($R^2 > 0.87$).

Manuscript in Prep (2026)

Assessment of Particulate and Gaseous Pollution and its Statistical Distribution: Barrackpore Municipality

Argha Kamal Guha, Enubothula Bala Mani, Piyush Raj, Navnit Kumar, Saptarshi Sadhu, Snehamanju Basu

Analyzed criteria pollutant probability distributions (Weibull, Log-normal, Gamma) and Kolmogorov-Smirnov goodness-of-fit in industrial suburban Kolkata.

Manuscript in Prep (2026)

Enhancing Micro Electrochemical Spark Machining of Exotic Non-Ferrous Materials through On-Process Electrolyte Sonication

Co-authors, Saptarshi Sadhu (4th Author)

Investigated ultrasonic sonication parameters on electrolyte flushing dynamics and spark discharge stability during micro-machining of non-ferrous alloys.
